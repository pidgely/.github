# Security policy

Please report vulnerabilities privately to **security@pidgely.com**. Do not open public issues for security problems.

Include a description, the affected component and version, reproduction steps and the impact you observed. We acknowledge reports within 3 business days and keep you updated until the issue is resolved. We do not take legal action against good-faith research that respects user privacy, avoids service disruption and gives us reasonable time to fix the issue before disclosure.

See also https://pidgely.com/.well-known/security.txt
